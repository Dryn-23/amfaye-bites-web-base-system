# Asset notes

The visual identity, inline sprout mark, green/cream layout and application UI were created for this project. No fast-food company's branding or official GCash UI is reproduced.

- `frontend/public/hero.png`: AI-generated illustrative croissant-and-shakes hero photograph.
- `frontend/public/products/mango.jpg` and `strawberry.jpg`: AI-generated illustrative product photography.
- Other sample menu photos are bundled illustrative images downloaded from Unsplash's image CDN. They are not photos of this business and some are shared across related sample products. Replace them with your own accurate, approved menu photography before operating a real store.
- Banana-bread placeholder currently uses the generated hero image; replace it with an actual banana-bread photo for real product use.
- DM Sans and Manrope are self-hosted Google Fonts, with their SIL Open Font License files bundled under `frontend/public/fonts`.
- Lucide icons are provided by the `lucide-react` package under its published ISC license.
